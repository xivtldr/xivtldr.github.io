## Hemitheos: Hegemone

:::tank

**TB**: Synergy (both)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Hemitheos's Dark IV
:::

---

- **Aetheric Polyominoid**: Avoid pulsating squares.
  - **Aetherial Exchange / Polyominoid Sigma**: Tethered pairs will swap locations. Look for pole that creates two safe zones.
  - Can combine with **Strophe Ixou**: CW/CCW rotating AoE. Resolve poles first.
- **Choros Ixou**: Hourglass Conals in direction of hands.
  - Can combine with **Aetheric Polyominoid / Exchange**.
- **Transmission**: Delayed Conal from players' position. Face outside when timer runs out.
