## Dæmoniac Dungeon: Pandæmonium

:::tank

**TB**: <mark>Multi</mark> Soul Grasp (shared), Wicked Step (Towers)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Ultima (DoT), <mark>L, Multi</mark> Harrowing Hell

**Doom (Esuna)**: Pandæmonic Pillars (Towers, when hit by AoE)
:::

---

- **Silkspit**: Break tethers with distance. Failure gives stacking debuff, 5 stacks kill.
  - Free trapped players by running through web.
  - Poison fields will get web bridge in later cast. Safe to cross.
- **Pandæmonic Pillars**: Towers, followed by Out (cage) or Donut (lasers) beneath them.
- **Pandæmonic Meltdown**: Line Stack
- **Pandæmonic Ray**: Half-Room into indicated direction.
- **Parted Plumes**: Sequential Conals covering entire arena.
  - Stand on last, move into first.
- **Touchdown**: Expanding AoE covering entire platform. Move across web bridge.
