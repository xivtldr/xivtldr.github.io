## Mythic Creation: Kokytos

:::tank

**TB**: Ascendant Fist, Gluttonous Rampage (stretch tether, go NW/NE)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Gluttony's Augur, Global Spell (DoT)
:::

---

- **Fire III**: Expanding Spread. Move to edge of arena.
- **Blizzard III**: Shrinking centered Donut.
  - **Dualspell**: Combines with **Fire III**. Only element flashed on screen will expand/shrink.
- **Archaic Rockbreaker**: Center Knockback.
  - Lava lines explode in Circle AoE. Stand in biggest gap.
- **Front/Rear Combination**: Donut or Out into Half-Room.
  - Front → stay behind, Rear → stay in front of boss.
- **Ecliptic Meteor**: Meteor, hide behind uncracked rock. Rock will explode afterwards.
- **Iceflame Summoning**: One element will flash on screen. Matching rocks will expand into large Circle AoE.
