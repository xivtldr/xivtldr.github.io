## Mythic Creation: The Phoinix

:::tank

**TB**: Heat of Condemnation (both)
:::

:::healer
_Second tank will receive auto-attacks after Add phase._

**RW**: Scorched Exaltation, Blazing Rain (Adds, repeated), Flames of Undeath, Dead Rebirth

:::

---

- **Experimental Fireplume**
  - Large Orb: Out
  - Small Orbs: Rotating Circle AoE's on edge. Start center, move out when center AoE appears
- **Darkened Fire / Brightened Fire**: Marked players must cover one fire each.
- **Sunbirds** Adds
  - Each cleaves front or back with Conal
  - Do not overlap birds on death. Circles can touch.
- **Fledgling Flight**: Sequential Conals in order of spawn. Position on last, move into first.
- **Devouring Brand**: Centered Plus, larger than expected.
