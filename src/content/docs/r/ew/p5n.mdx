## Mythic Creation: Proto-Carbuncle

:::tank

**TB**: Crunch, Toxic Crunch
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Sonic Howl, <mark>L</mark> Ruby Glow
:::

---

:::all
**Ruby Glow**: Walls can be walked through. Every **magic** attack (indicated by glowing staves) hitting a wall will affect the segmented area in which the mechanic went off.
:::

- **Searing Ray**: Get behind boss (with wall: get to opposite segment of boss).
- **Topaz Stones**
  - 2/3 Stones: Delayed explosion of segmented square.
  - 4 Stones: Move into square of last spawned stone, then into other segment once its safe.
  - Poison Stones: Expanding Circle AoE.
  - Can Combo with **Searing Ray**.
- **Starving Stampede**: Memorize order. Stand in 4th jump location, move into first.
