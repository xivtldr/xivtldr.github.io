## Hemitheos: Agdistis

:::tank

**TB**: Hemitheos's Aero II (both)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Spark of Life (DoT)
:::

---

- **Bough of Attis**:
  - Hands close to chest: Move to back of arena. Followed by large Line AoE.
  - Hands over arena: Move to front of arena.
- **Hemitheos's Holy / Hemitheos's Glare III**: Spread into Center Donut.
- **Forbidden Fruit**: Untargetable Adds.
  - Immature Io: Circle AoE covering entire platform they stand on.
  - Immature Stymphalide: Line AoE in direction they are facing.
  - Both adds, **Bough of Attis**, Towers, Knockback combine in pairs later on.
- **Blades of Attis**: Two [Exaflares](glossary:exaflare).
  - Platform Change: Donut into three [Exaflares](glossary:exaflare).
