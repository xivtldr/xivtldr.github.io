## Ephemeral Justice: Themis

:::tank

**TB**: Dike (each), Upheld Ruling (if purple, huge Circle)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Eunomia (DoT), Emissary's Will

**Styx**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker
:::

---

:::all
**Gold/Purple Rings**: Move **close to (gold)** or **away from (purple)** boss.

Always prepare for follow-up ring mechanics.
:::

- **Divisive Ruling**: Center Line AoE. Mind ring color for follow-up.
- **Dark and Light / Sigils of Discord**: Stand in color opposite to your debuff.
  - Combines with **Dineis**: Baited AoE, stack towards front of your circle.
  - Later combines with **Dismissal Ruling**.
- **Dismissal Ruling**: Knockback with Donut or Out based on ring color.
- **Upheld Ruling**
  - Purple: Tankbuster turns into Donut.
  - Gold: Stack Marker into Out.
- **Shadowed Messenger**: Large Plus across arena.
  - Move to the side of the purple clone, then move to where its front was for the follow-up.
