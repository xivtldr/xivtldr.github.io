## Perfect Imperfection: Hephaistos

:::tank

**TB**: Flameviper
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Genesis of Flame, <mark>L</mark> Gorgoneion (Gorgons), Rearing Rampage, Ektothermos
:::

---

- **Sunforge**
  - Dragon: Sides are safe
  - Firebird: Center is safe
- **Reforged Reflection**
  - **Blazing Footfalls**: Sequential Knockbacks. Position towards next Knockback.
  - **Into the Shadows**: Avoid two Gaze Markers once they stop.
- **Fourfold Fires / Cthonic Vent**: Tethered puddles will explode in large Circle AoE's. Remaining puddles will explode after birds moved into them.
- **Volcanic Torches**: Determine safe-spot on first direction change.
  - Later combines with Spread, **Fourfold Fires / Cthonic Vent**.
  - Later combines with **Reforged Reflection / Into the Shadows**
