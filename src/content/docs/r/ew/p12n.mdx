## Theos: Athena

:::tank

**TB**: Glaukopis (Line)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: On the Soul, Ultima Blades, Theos's Ultima

_Add phase will have unavoidable [Charges](glossary:charge)._
:::

---

- **Trinity of Souls**: Memorize order of glowing wings. [Half-Rooms](glossary:half-room).
- **Paradeigma**: Line AoE's from all Adds.
- **Parthenos**: Centered Line AoE.
- **Superchain Theory**
  - Move to vortex chained to Donut (blue).
  - Shortest chained vortex will go off first.
  - Later cast combines with **Parthenos**.
  - Later cast combines with **Paradeigma** and **Unnatural Enchainment** - no Donut vortex.
- **Unnatural Enchainment**: Move away from tethered arena rectangles.
- Add phase: Targeted AoE Circles, 270° Conal, unavoidable [Charges](glossary:charge)
  - Center four panels will disappear after add phase.
