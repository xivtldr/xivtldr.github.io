## Lamebrix Strikebocks

:::tank
_Tank boss towards the South wall._

**TB**: <mark>Multi</mark> Goblin Rush, <mark>L, Multi</mark> Gobrush Rushgob

:::

:::healer

**RW**: Gobsway Rumblerocks

:::

---

### Buttons

Buttons will periodically start glowing. Don't stand on them, or mechanics activate. Boss can jump onto them.

- Six Drops (S): Loss of control
- Star (Center): Three large spike balls drop into center
- Crystal (N): Line AoE on both sides, stand in center

### Main mechanics

- **Prey**: Distance tether. Do not stand near buttons, as boss will jump to your position.
- **Single Charge**: Green is Donut, Red is Out
