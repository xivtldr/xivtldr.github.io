## Boss gauntlet, Brute Justice

:::tank
_Brawler & Vortexer, Swindler & Blaster: One tank per boss._

- **TB**:
  - Onslaughter: Hydrothermal Missile, Perpetual Ray (Magic Vuln Up)
  - Brawler & Vortexer: Single Barrel Arm
  - Brute Justice: <mark>L</mark> Double Rocket Punch (shared)

:::

:::healer

- **RW**:
  - Onslaughter: Add explode in RW and give Vuln Up if not killed in time
  - Swindler & Blaster: Mindblast
  - Brute Justice: Short Needle, J Kick

:::

---

### Boss gauntlet

- **Brawler & Vortexer**: Double Barrel Arm is a Stack Marker!
- **Swindler & Blaster**: Check your debuff. Always stand on a platform opposite to your debuff.
  - **→ Purple stand on Low, Red stand on High ←**

### Brute Justice

- **Super Jump**: Knockback for anyone standing near targeted player
- Swindler & Brawler will appear and repeat their mechanic (High/Low, Stack)
