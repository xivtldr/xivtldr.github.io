## Ratfinx Twinkledinks

:::tank

Boss will give MT stacks of debuff _Headache_. 4 Stacks of Headache result in uncleansable debuff _Concussion_, rendering MT unable to move while greatly increasing damage taken.

**Off-tank**: Provoke before Tankbuster, right after MT gets _Concussion_

**TB**: Kaltstrahl (Fausts), Gobstraight (OT Provoke)

:::

:::healer

**RW**: Panzerschreck, Gobswing (players not transformed into bird)
:::

:::all
Standing in Center Pole in the room activates purple and red puddle, needed for mechanics.

- Puddles transform, use action _Apothecary_ to turn back
  - Red: Gorilla , needed to kick bombs away
  - Purple: Bird, needed for Gobswing

:::

---

- **Bomb's Away**: Massive Bombs dropping into the arena
  - Gorilla (Red) must kick them away from raid with _Browbeat_
- **Boost**, **Gobswing**: Turn into bird to survive next attack
  - Transform into bird (purple). Already transformed player must stand in Center to keep puddles active.
