## Boss gauntlet: Blaster, Brawler, Swindler, Vortexer

:::tank

**TB**:

- Blaster: Brute Force
- Brawler: Magicked Mark (Magic Vuln Up), Single Barrel Arm
  - Double Barrel Arm is a Stack Marker! Move to the party to share damage
- Swindler: Magicked Mark
- Vortexer: Brute Force (Phys Vuln Up)
  :::

:::healer

**RW**: Mind Blast (Blaster), Bio-Arithmeticks (Swindler)

_Mind Brawler's Magicked Mark and Vortexer's Brute Force, which give the tank stacking Magic/Phys Vuln Ups._
:::

---

### Brawler

- Double Barrel Arm: Untelegraphed Stack Marker

### Swindler

- Check your debuff. Always stand on a platform opposite to your debuff.
  - **→ Purple stand on Low, Red stand on High ←**
- Circle with _n_ orbs: Stack Marker requiring _n_ players

### Vortexer

- **Elemental Jammer**: Check if you got debuff _Compressed Water_. Move away, as Water Spout will spawn beneath you in 21s.
- Ice Marker: One player must overlap their circle with a Water Spout
  - This creates an Ice Boulder
- Ultra Flash: Meteor, hide behind Ice Boulder
