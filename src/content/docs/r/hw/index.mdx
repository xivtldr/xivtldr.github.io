_Alexander_ is the Level 60 8-man raid series of Heavensward. It was released in
three tiers: Gordias (Patch 3.01), Midas (Patch 3.2) and The Creator (Patch
3.4). Tiers are referred to as _A\_N_.

The raid series is unlocked with the blue quest _Disarmed_ (NPC _Slowfix_, Idyllshire [X:7.5, Y:6.5]).
