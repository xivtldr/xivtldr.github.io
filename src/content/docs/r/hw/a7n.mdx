## Quickthinx Allthoughts

:::tank

**TB**: <mark>Multi</mark> Uplander Doom (Phys Vuln Up, hits increase as the fight progresses)

_If your MT is undergeared, consider tank swapping if MT has too many stacks._
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Sizzlespark, Kugelblitz (Sturm Doll Add, Paralysis), Self Destruct (Adds, if not killed in time)
:::

---

- Exhaust Ducts: Raid splits left and right, one healer, one tank per side
- **Sizzlebeam**: Line AoE, stay away from raid
- **Zoomdoom**: Boss will grab anyone near marked player, stay away
- Stun Sturm Doll Add when its casting **Kugelblitz**
- Cat / **True Heart**: Kill Heart, heals and buffs boss if it reaches him
  - Pairs with **Weight of the World**: Avoid giant steel ball
  - Pairs with tethered bombs: Affected players must move into one of four corners

### Four cages

- Red, Fire: Pyretic, don't move/performe action. Players outside must break the padlock.
- Purple, Electric: Throttle (Doom debuff), kill add before timer runs out
- White, Ice: Don't touch walls (mind Knockback!), kill add
- Green, Poison: Stand on top of gas leak, cage will lower on its own
