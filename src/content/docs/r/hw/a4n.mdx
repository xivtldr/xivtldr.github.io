## The Manipulator

:::tank

**TB**: Hydrothermal Missile
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Mortal Revolution, Carnage Zero (Adds, last phase)

**Judgement Nisi**: DoT put on healers. Can be passed on to someone else by standing close to them.
**Do not stand close to another player with this debuff, or both of you will die**.
:::

:::all
All attacks are dealing Splash damage. Spread out.

Focus on one leg.
:::

---

### First Leg

- Blue/Orange Orbs: Orange orbs drift towards player with Blue Orb.
  - Other players must absorb orbs by running through them.
- **Emergency Quarantine**: One DPS and Off-tank, defeat the add
  - Don't die or you can't be revived.

### Second Leg

- Tethers: Intercept to reduce damage done to targeted player
- Jagd Dolls: Kill asap.
  - Target players. If they reach them, they will explode and give everyone nearby the debuff _Luminous Aetheroplasm_.
  - 4 Stacks of Luminous Aetheroplasm result in instant death

### The Manipulator

- Adds will cast Carnage Zero: Chaining AoE, spread out
- **Judgement Nisi**: Blue and red debuff put on healers
  - If you have a colored debuff **DO NOT STAND CLOSE TO ANOTHER PLAYER WITH THIS DEBUFF**
  - Debuff starts with 5 decreasing stacks. Last few stacks deal high damage
  - It can be passed on to someone else by standing close to them
