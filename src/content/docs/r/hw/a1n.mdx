## Faust, Oppressor

:::tank
_Second Oppressor is meant to be tanked away from the first one. Not relevant anymore, just burn both down._

**TB**: Kaltstrahl (Faust, Cleaving), Adds (Faust), Royal Fount (untelegraphed)

**OT**: Pick up Faust's Adds.
:::

:::healer
_Hydrothermal Missile can't be dodged. Be prepared to heal the target._

**RW**: Gunnery Pod, 3000-Onze Missile (scales if not correctly solved)

:::

---

- Focus Faust
- **Hydrothermal Missile**: Multiple missiles targeting one player, can't be dodged
- **Emergency Deployment**: Puddles left behind reduce damage dealt and increase damage taken
- **3000-Onze Missile**: Kill adds directly under laser. Puddles shrink the missiles, reducing damage
  - You probably won't see this mechanic
- Burn both Oppressors down
  - Last Oppressor standing will start **Self Destruct** Enrage
