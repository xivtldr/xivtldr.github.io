## Living Liquid

:::tank
**TB**: Fluid Swing, Orange Tether (Water Spout, cleave)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: <mark>Multi</mark> Splash, Severe Contamination (Water Spout), Adds (Water Spout, on death), Cascade
:::

---

### Hand Form

- **Wash Away**: Large Knockback (direction is random!)

#### Water Spout Form

_Usually skipped / not seen._

- Kill adds before they reach the outside wall!
  - Any add that reaches the wall inflicts Vuln Up and Dropsy
  - Pistole Lubricants will sprint once they come near the wall. Focus them down first.
- Orange Tether: Pass to the Tank
- Magnets
  - Same Polarity: Stand close to each other, in the center. Knockback.
  - Different Polarity: Stand far away from each other, to the side of Spout. Pull.

### Last Phase

- Magnets: See above
