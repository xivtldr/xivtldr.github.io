## Party Animal: Dancing Green

:::tank

**TB**: Deep Cut (both, Conal)

:::

:::healer

**RW**: Disco Infernal, Celebrate Good Times, Let's Pose!

:::

:::all
Melee DPS: No positional requirements while boss has _Directional Disregard_ debuff.
:::

---

- **2/4-snap Twist**: Half-Room, repeats twice or four times, with a final hit on opposite side
- **Disco Infernal**: Gives two _Burn Baby Burn_ debuffs, lasting 17s and 35s
  - When countdown appears / debuff runs out, stand in spotlight. Repeats a second time.
- **Ride the Waves**: Traveling Line AoE, move to gap. Two sets.
  - Combines with **2/4-snap Twist**
- **Ensemble Assemble**: Disco Frogs
  - **Arcady Night Fever**: Half-Room one after another. Frogs show which half will be hit in order.
- **Disco Infernal** (2nd): Debuffs last 10s and 44s, one less spotlight
  - Also combines with moonwalking frogs (painting AoE), **2/4-snap Twist**
