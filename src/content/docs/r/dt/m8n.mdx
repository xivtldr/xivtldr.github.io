## Lone Wolf: The Howling Blade

:::tank
_Adds don't have to be separated._

**TB**: Great Divide (shared)

:::

:::healer

**RW**: Extraplanar Pursuit, <mark>Multi</mark> Ravenous Saber

**Tracking Tremors**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker

:::

---

- **Wolves' Reign**: Clones create Circle AoE at indicated edge of arena
  - Followed by Line AoE from boss position and 120° Conal
- **Beckon Moonlight**: Four sequential Half-Room Cleaves, indicated by clones' sword
- **Shadowchase**: Stand behind clones
- **Bare Fangs**: Two sets of Line AoE's, originating from lasers
- After Ultimate, all attacks will resolve faster
- **Terrestrial Titans**: Watch for slice indicating direction of fall
  - One pillar will always fall into the center of arena
  - Stand in front of this pillar, then move to left for safe zone
- **Bare Fangs** (2nd): Now combines with **Wolves' Reign** and **Shadowchase**
