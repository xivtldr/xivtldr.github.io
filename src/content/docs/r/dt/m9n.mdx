## Sweet-toothed Sadist: Vamp Fatale

:::tank

**TB**: Hardcore (both, humungous in last phase)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Killer Voice, Sadistic Screech, <mark>L</mark> Crowd Kill, Finale Fatale, Insatiable Thirst

**Brutal Rain**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker
:::

---

- **Half Moon**: Half-Room in the direction of orb, immediately followed by cleave in opposite direction
  - Covers almost 2/3rd after first Add phase
- **Vamp Stomp**: Bats hit by expanding circle explode in Circle AoE
- Coffinmaker (Add)
  - **Penetrating Pitch**: Light Party Split Stack Marker. Do not overlap.
  - Sequential Line AoE combines with Half-Room
- **Aetherletting**: Targeted Circle AoE's, leaving behind Plus and X AoE's
- **Sadistic Screech** (2nd): Large sawblades traveling E-W, small sawblades traveling N-S. DoT when hit.
  - Towers leave behind Spike Bomb. Kill asap.
  - Combines with RW's
