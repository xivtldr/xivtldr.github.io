## Twisted Vengeance: Brute Abombinator

:::tank

**TB**: Brutish Swing (shared)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: <mark>Multi</mark> Brutal Impact, Neo Bombarian Special, Revenge of the Vines, Powerslam

:::

---

### Phase 1

- **Stoneringer: The Bludgeoning (club) / The Stabbing (sword)**: Out / Donut
- Adds will create 3 Plus AoE's, 1 Donut. Interrupt.
- **Quarry Swamp**: Meteor, hide behind add.
  - Proximity markers will go off in order of appearance, followed by **Stoneringer**
- **Neo Bombarian Special**: Stand as close to northern edge as possible

### Phase 2

- **Stoneringer: The Stabbing**: Boss now jumps to tethered wall before performing Donut. Boss can also store a Donut for later use
  - **Sploresploshion**: 3 sets of sequential Circular AoE. Immediately followed by stored Donut.

### Phase 3

- **Stoneringer 2: Stoneringers**: Pay attention to tether (location) and glowing weapon (club Out, sword Donut)
- **Lashing Lariat**: Fast-casting Half-Room, indicated by glowing arm
