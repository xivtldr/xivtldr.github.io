## Glory Incarnate: The Lindwurm

:::tank
**TB**: Visceral Burst
:::

:::healer
_Both tanks receive auto-attacks._

**RW**: The Fixer, Splattershed

:::

---

- **Ravenous Reach**: Large Conal in direction of serpent head
  - Leaves behind expanding Circle AoE
- **Grotesquerie** (2nd): Knockback or Push in indicated direction
  - Also gives some players purple debuff: Delayed Conal
- **Mindless Flesh**: Five sequential Line AoE's
  - Followed by huge AoE. Only small column, opposite from final Line AoE, will be safe.
