## Neoteric Witch: Wicked Thunder

:::tank

**TB**: Wicked Jolt
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Wrath of Zeus

**Wicked Bolt**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker

**Wicked Hypercannon**: <mark>Multi</mark> Line Stack (last phase, soft-enrage)
:::

---

- **Shadows' Sabbath**: Clones cast Half-Room AoE's
- North Edge Jump, Cannon: Multiple large, full-length Aoe's in 2/3rd's of arena
  - Watch for cannon orientation and move to furthest 1/3rd
- **n-fold Blast**: Sequential Line AoE in indicated direction, memorize order
  - Orange diamond will hit front, blue circle will hit back
- **Bewitching Flight**: Stand between lasers
  - Followed by wide Line AoE's, indicated by glow
- **Witch Hunt**: Inwards or outwards traveling Circle AoE
  - Wait for first circles to go off, then move into them
