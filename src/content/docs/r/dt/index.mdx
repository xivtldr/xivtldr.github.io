_The Arcadion (AAC)_ is the Level 100 8-man raid series of Dawntrail. It was
released in three tiers: Light-heavyweight Tier (Patch 7.01), Cruiserweight Tier
(Patch 7.2) and Heavyweight Tier (Patch 7.4). Tiers are referred to as _M\_N_.

The raid series is unlocked with the blue quest _A New Challenger Appears_ (NPC _Metem_, Solution Nine [X:19.1, Y:18.8]).
