## Iron Fist: The Tyrant

:::tank
**TB**: Raw Steel (shared), Foregone Fatality (intercept tether), Great Wall of Fire (shared)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Crown of Arcadia, <mark>Multi</mark> Dance of Domination, <mark>1HP</mark> Charybdistopia, <mark>L</mark> One and Only, Flatliner

**Heartbreak Kick**: <mark>Multi</mark> Tower + Stack Marker.
:::

---

### Draw Steel / Smashdown

- Scythe: Donut
- Axe: Out
- Sword: Plus, relative to where he is facing
- **Trophy Weapons / Assault Evolved**: Sequential weapon attacks, order indicated by tether
  - **Ultimate Trophy Weapons**: No tethers, memorize spawn order. Wind Vortexes will explode in sequential telegraphed Conals.

### Main mechanics

- **Dance of Domination**: White gashes will explode. Centers of large triangles are safe.
- **Double Tyrannihilation**: Meteor, hide behind **rock directy behind another rock**
- **Flatliner**: Split into Light Parties. Mind Knockback.
- **Fire and Fury**: Proximity Marker with centered 90° Conal
- **Arcadion Avalanche**: Platform boss is facing will be destroyed. Use Knockback Tower to switch platform.
