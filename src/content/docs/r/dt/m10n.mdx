## Daring Devils: The Xtremes (Red Hot and Deep Blue)

:::tank
**TB**: Hot Impact (shared), Deep Impact, <mark>Multi, L</mark> Xtreme Spectacular
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Divers' Dare (both bosses in P2), Firesnaking / Watersnaking

:::

---

:::all
Fire attacks leave behind persistent fire fields, shrinking the available space. Fire fields hit by water attacks will explode in slightly larger than expected Circle AoE (P2) - watch for orbs appearing.
:::

- **Cutback Blaze**: Baited Conal on two players
- **Sick Swell** / **Sickest Take-off**: Knockback with Center Line AoE
  - Don't get knocked into lingering fire field.
- **Deep Varial**: Wide Conal originating from small wave
- **Firesnaking / Watersnaking**: Colored tethers and debuff indicate which boss targets you. Stay with your boss.
  - **Insane Air**: Bosses will jump to marked position
  - Marked players must bait Conal away from that position
  - **Water Conals should avoid hitting lingering fire fields**
