## Sweet Poison: Honey B. Lovely

:::tank

**TB**: Honeyed Breeze (both, Conal)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Call Me Honey, Honey B. Live

:::

---

- **Tempting Twist** / **Honey Beeline**: Donut, Wide Line AoE
- **Honey B. Live**: Every mechanic hit will fill heart meter
  - 1 and 2 hearts give DoTs, 3 hearts lead to loss of control
  - **Love Me Tender** (2nd): Shiva Circles
- After this phase **Tempting Twist** / **Honey Beeline** will also spawn fast-casting Circle AoE below blobs of honey
- **Alarm Pheromones**: Sequential Line AoE's followed by Line AoE's
- **Honey B. Live** (2nd): Now includes Light Party Split Stack Marker
- **Alarm Pheromones**: (2nd): Now targets random players instead
