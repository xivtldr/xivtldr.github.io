## Art at War: Sugar Riot

:::tank

**TB**: Color Riot (both)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Mousse Mural

**Pudding Party**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker

_Desert landscape will put DoT on everyone while phase lasts._
:::

---

### **Single Style**

- Paint **Bomb**: Circle AoE, larger than expected
- Heaven Bomb (**wings**): Circle AoE on opposite side
- Sweet Shot (**arrows**): Crossing Line AoE
- Throw-up Target (**arrow in target**): Get to narrow side of Bullseye
- **Double Style** combines two styles

### Desert landscape

- Cacti: Large Circle AoE. Multiple sets, order indicated by growth.
- Quicksand: Go to corners. Combines with Cacti

### River landscape

- Standing in water increases Electric damage, reduces Fire damage.
  - Fireball: Light Party Split Stack Marker
  - Lightning Bolts: Spread on grass
- Thunderstorm: Avoid river. **Tempest Piece** cloud will cast massive Circle AoE.
