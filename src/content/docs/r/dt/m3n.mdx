## Vile Heat: Brute Bomber

:::tank

**TB**: Knuckle Slandwich (shared)
:::

:::healer

**RW**: <mark>Multi</mark> Brutal Impact (4 hits, upgraded to 6 after Doping Draught)

**Esuna**: Concussion (after 2 stacks of Trauma)
:::

---

- **Murderous Mist**: 270° Conal, behind boss is safe
- **Barbarous Barrage**: Towers with Knockback, one player is enough.
- **Fuses of Fury**: Locate bomb with long fuse, move into exploded areas
- **Doping Draught** will now augment some attacks
  - **Lariat Combo**: Now hits twice
  - **Infernal Spin**: Now has sequential Out into Donut
  - **Fuses of Fury**: Now combines with **Lariat Combo** and **Barbarous Barrage** (position towards long fuse bomb)
