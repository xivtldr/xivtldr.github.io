## Claw in the Dark: Black Cat

:::tank

**TB**: Biscuit Maker
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Bloody Scratch

**Overshadow**: Line Stack, repeats 4x

:::

---

- **Black Cat Crossing**: Sequential Plus/Intercardinal Conals (or vice versa)
- **Mouser**: Memorize flashed squares and order
  - Prioritize memorizing which square gets hit twice
  - Ranged can camp on one of two southern central squares, one will not get hit
- **Elevate and Eviscerate**: Move arc until it turns yellow, do not land on cracked square
- **Predaceous Pounce**: Wait next to first circle, then move into it when it goes off
  - Later cast by clone instead, while boss repeats other mechanics
- **Leaping One-two Paw**: Black Cat will move to marker and perform sequential Half-Room on new position
- **Leaping Black Cat Crossing**: Same as One-two Paw, but with Sequential Plus/Intercardinal Conals
