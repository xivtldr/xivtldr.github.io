    - __Colossal Impact__
        - Glowing red mouth: Sides are safe
        - Boss moves back: Center is safe
    - __Replicate__: Cubes show order.
        - Clones also cast Colossal Impact
    - __Spheroids__: Alternating Line AoE when paired with _Magical Artillery Alpha_.
    - __Boxes__: Use to avoid knockbacks. Mind simultaneous Colossal Impact.

  <TabItem label="Hansel & Gretel">
    :::tank
      _Separate both bosses._
    :::

    :::healer
      _TB and Raidwide swaps between bosses._

      __RW__: Wail, **<mark>L</mark>** Lamentation (when one boss is down)
    :::

    ___
    _Shielded boss: Reflects damage unless attacked from front_

    - __Bloody Sweep__: Cleave from glowing lances
        - Lances are outside: Between bosses is safe
        - Lances are inside: Opposite of arena is safe
        - Arrows between them: Safe zones swap
    - __Passing Lance__: Stand at edge, to the side of opening
    - __Breakthrough__: Cleave, stand on edge opposite of lances
    - __Hungry Lance__: Conal AoE, side of boss is safe

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Red Girl">
    :::tank
      __TB__: Manipulate Energy (each tank, splash)
    :::

    :::healer
      __RW__: **<mark>L</mark>** Cruelty
    :::

    ___

    #### Color mechanic

    - __Shockwave__: Position circle next to blocks with __opposite__ color
    - Hide from line AoE by standing behind block with __same__ color
    ___
    - __Replicate__: 120° traveling Conal in direction of small Red Girls.
        - Stand next to a RG, and move into each second Conal that goes off.

    #### Minigame
    - Use F-0 Switch to change laser and drone color.
    - Opposite color destroys
    :::deadly
      - Same color protects from AoE.
    :::

    - __Child's Play__: Forced march into indicated direction. __Do not use Sprint__.
    - __Replicate (P2)__: Small Red Girls will be moved into indicated direction.
        - One spot won't have a Red Girl.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Falls Idol, Her Inflorescence">
    :::tank
      __TB__: Darker Note (each tank, circular AoE)
    :::

    :::healer
      __RW__: Screaming Score, Eminence
    :::

    ___
    - __Made Magic__: Half-Width Cleave in direction of turning wheel
    - __Recreate Structure__: AoE from below the floor
    - __Mixed Signals__: Stand in line of red signal
    - __Heavy Arms__
        - Pillar Arms: Center is safe
        - Pillar: Side is safe

    - __The Final Song__: Colored AoE circle
        - Match and point color of your ring towards it
    - __Towerfall__: Falling pillars, shadows can be misleading

  </TabItem>
