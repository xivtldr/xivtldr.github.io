**YoRHa: Dark Apocalypse**, a collaboration with _NieR: Automata_, is the Level
80 24-man raid series of Shadowbringers. It was released in three parts: The
Copied Factory (Patch 5.1), The Puppets' Bunker (Patch 5.3) and The Tower at
Paradigm's Breach (Patch 5.5).

The series is unlocked with the blue quest _Word about Komra_ (NPC _Gossipy Dwarf_, Kholusia [X:12.9, Y:8.7]).
