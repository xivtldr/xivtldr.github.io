<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Cetus">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Immersion
    :::

    - **Adds**: Players marked with green swirls must move away from raid
    - **Body Slam (2nd)**: Now followed by [Donut](glossary) or [Out](glossary)

  </TabItem>

<TabItem label="Forward Port Ventilator">

- Alliances split.
- Prioritize the Pagan's Knots.
- If not killed fast enough
  they will put up a poison cloud.
- Clear it with the lit up circle platforms
  (all three platforms need players in it).

</TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Irminsul and Sawtooth">
    :::tank
      _Both bosses need to be tanked, separate them._
    :::

    :::all
      **Bow buff**: Boss is resistant to range attacks.

      **Staff buff**: Boss is resistant to magic attacks.
    :::

    ___

    - **Mucus Bomb**: Move away from raid.
      - Anyone caught will be tethered. Move through tethers to free them.
    - **Mucus Spray** (Sawtooth): Donut. Same as Mucus Bomb.
    - **Black Circles**: Kill insects.
      - Anything in circles will be eaten after a few seconds, filling Enrage meter.
      - Loss of control and small [Conal](glossary) attacks if you get eaten.

    :::deadly
      **Shockwave Stomp**: Break Line of Sight with Sawtooth. Stand behind Irminsul.
    :::

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Cuchulainn">
    :::healer
      _Constantly stacking bleeding debuff. Can't be removed. Idol of Impurity adds inflict additional stacks._

      **RW**: Pestilence. Large damage if any adds were eaten by boss.
    :::

    ___

    - **Corrosive Bile**: Targeted Conal AoE.
    - **Flailing Tentacles**: Stand in-between tentacles.
    - **Idol of Impurity**: Destroy Gyrtowers.
    - **Void Pact**: Prioritize add targeted with a Conal AoE.
        - Boss gets damage up if attack hits the add.
    - **Pestilence**: Stand on raised platform.
    - **Black Lung**: Run through orbs.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Echidna">
    :::tank
      _Separate Adds._

      **TB**: Sickle Strike
    :::

    :::healer
      **RW**: Gehenna (after adds)
    :::

    ___

    - **Sickle Slash**: Center of boss is safe.
    - **Abyssal Reaper**: Out with [Knockback](glossary).
    - **Nemesis Burst**: Out with Knockback.

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
