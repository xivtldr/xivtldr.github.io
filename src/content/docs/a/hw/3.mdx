    ___

    - __Void Death__: Continuously run towards walls.
    - __Void Aero II__: Drop at the edges.
    - __Void Aero III__: Knockback, position yourself toward wall furthest away from boss.

    :::deadly
      - __Void Blizzard IV__: Move to other side of crystals.
        - Immediately followed by Knockback
        - Position yourself toward crystals or you will die.
    :::

  <TabItem label="Ferdiad Hollow">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Jongleur’s X
    :::

    :::healer
      **RW**: Flameflow (scales in damage with incorrectly colored fire/water spouts)
    :::

    ___
    - __Wormhole__
        - __Big blue orb__: Out.
        - __Golden Pretzel__: Donut.
        - Two attacks after add phase.

    - __Debilitator__: Fire Resistance Down or Water Resistance Down debuff
        - __Red (Fire) Debuff__: Walk through fire spouts.
        - __Water (Blue) Debuff__: Walk through water spouts.
    - When Ferdiad moves to center surrounded by Atomos, get out of their direct path.
    - __Jester's Reward__: Half-Width cleave immediately after adds.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Scathach">
    :::tank
      _Keep add away from boss_

      __TW__: Thirty Thorns (untelegraphed)
    :::

    :::healer
      **RW**: Thirty Souls, Blinding Shadow (after cocoon phase)
    :::

    ---
    - When she jumps up, get Out of melee range.
    - __Shadespin__: Hourclock AoE.
        - Raised Wing shows direction.
        - Sometimes followed up by a Dash.
    - __"Shadows gather on the floor"__: Don't move, sheath weapon.
    - __Shadethrust__: Line AoE.
    - Tethered add: Look at add. Don't let it reach you.
    - Chimera
        - Dragon's Voice: Donut. Ram's Voice: Out.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Diabolos">
    :::tank
      _Tower phase: Get into the center circle._

      **TB**: <mark>L</mark> Hollow Camisado. (will always crit)
    :::

    :::healer
      _Mind __Hollow Camisado__. TB with crit!_

      __RW__: Ruinous Omen, Ultimate (after Life Gate), Pavor Inanis, Hollow Omen

      __Doom (Esuna)__: Hollow Nightmare
    :::

    ___

    - __Ultimate Terror__: Donut
    - __Shadethrust__: Line AoE in front of boss.
    - [Stack marker](glossary) with Gaze: Face away from marked player.
    - Glowing Gate: Some players must move into it and kill an add.
    - Tower phase: Only tanks in center.
    - __Earth Shaker__: Conal AoE starting from boss position towards marked players.
