# The Weeping City of Mhach

_Shadow of Mhach: Second Heavensward 24-man alliance raid_

<div data-pagefind-ignore>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Arachne Eve">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Dark Spike
    :::

    :::healer
      **RW**: Implosion
    :::

    ___

    - **Burrow under the ground** Expanding AoE's traveling outwards.
        - Followed by <u>proximity marker</u>.
    - **Green AoE**: Move away from raid.
        - Tethered players can be freed by running through tethers.
    - **Webs above arena**: Avoid green areas.
        - Intercept orbs before they reach the boss.
        - Kill spiders, jump through holes, kill pillars and add.
    - **Yellow marker**: Move away from others.
        - Anyone close by will be pulled up to web area.
    - **Red Marker**: Drop at edge of arena. Creates web patches.
    - **Vortex**: Stand in web patches or you get sucked in.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Forgall">
    :::tank
      _Separate adds._
    :::

    :::healer
      _If zombies aren't killed fast enough they will explode, resulting in raidwide damage._

      **RW**: Punishing Ray, Evil Mist, Mana Explosion (after adds), **<mark>L</mark>** Hell Wind

      **Doom (Esuna)**: Mortal Ray (Haagenti)
    :::

    ___
    - Move away from zombies. Kill them once they move.
    - **Necropurge, Evil Mist**: Avoid, gives debuff.
        - 2 stacks result in loss of control.
    - **Brand of the Fallen**: <u>Kiss</u>
    - **Adds**: Succubus (Interrupt cast) > Haagenti

    :::deadly
      :skull: **Mega Death**: Stand in green circle. Avoid overlapping areas.
    :::

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Headstone">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Trembling Epigraph, Big Burst (if adds are not killed)
    :::

    ---

    - Alliances split.
    - Kill adds.
    - **Flaring Epigraph**: Walk over circles on each platform to raise shield
    - Hide inside shielded area or you die.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Ozma">
    :::tank
      _Grab adds appearing after proximity attack. Try to grab blue orbs._

      - **TB**: (Cube form) Straight line attacks after Donut.
          - Keep distance from party.
    :::

    :::healer
      _Blue orbs need to be grabbed & deal high damage - shield when possible._
    :::

    ___

    #### Forms
    - **Sphere**: Red marker - drop on segment to the left. Do not stack.
        - Others can stack up on boss.
    - __Pyramid__: Move to the sides.
        - Can be paired with <u>Acceleration Bomb</u>
    - __Cube__: <u>Donut</u>
        - Move away from tank after donut
        - Only grab orbs w/o vuln stacks, enough health.
    ___
    - Add phase: <u>Gaze</u>
        - If hit, stop attacking and moving to cleanse debuff.
        - If debuff is not cleansed, players become jailed.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Calofisteri">
    :::tank
      _Tank her in center of arena._
    :::

    :::healer
      __RW__: <mark>Multi</mark> Dancing Mad
    :::

    ___

    - __Haircut__: <u>Half-Width</u> Cleave.
        - Watch for hair with a scythe, indicating the side to be hit.

    - **Flowers**: Move close to the axe flowers (<u>Donut</u>).
        - Avoid being in the line of scythes.
    - **Penetration**: Look away and position yourself facing space between two flower seeds.
        - Look away: <u>Knockback</u>, Look at her: <u>Pull</u>.
    - __Depth Charge__: Don't stand in front.
        - Immediately followed by _Haircut_.

  </TabItem>
