**Shadow of Mhach** is the Level 60 24-man raid series of Heavensward. It was released in three parts: The Void Ark (Patch 3.1), The Weeping City of Mhach (Patch 3.3) and Dun Scaith (Patch 3.5).

The series is unlocked with the blue quest _Sky Pirates_ (NPC _Unquiet Trader_, Ishgard/The Pillars [X:14.1, Y:10.7]).
