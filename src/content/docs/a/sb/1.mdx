    - __Unbind__: A dancer moves along the spiral
        - Aqua Sphere: Kill before dancer touches it
        - Gather two of the blue orbs, stand off the spiral

        - Any Blizzard III need to be destroyed asap

    - __Suffocation__: Breathless Debuff
        - Kill Flume Toads, they drop air pockets
        - Do not let Breathless Debuff hit 10 stacks. Use air pockets to drop stacks.
        - Targeted Line AoE: Point away from air pockets

    - __Extreme Edge__: Half-Width Cleave
    - __Command Tower__: Kill adds. Followed by Ultimate.
    - __Summon__: Two adds, damage up every time they grow.
    - __Submission Tower__: Kill adds, one for each alliance.
        - Do not stack the stack marker (magic vuln up).

    :::healer
      __Esuna__: Crush Helm (stacks 4 times)

    ___
    - __Chariot__: Line AoE. If marked, run close to the boss.
    - __Cry of Victory__: Large cleave, directly after Chariot and Trample. Pre-move near boss.
    - __Trample__: Figure 8. Upper/Lower part of 8 and outside is safe.
    - __Three clones__: Create Line AoE. Move to first clone that spawns in for safe zone.
    - __Three Adds__: Alliance split. Stand towards centre of arena.
    - __Heavenly Subjugation__: Ultimate, less damage when standing on the side.
    - __Embrace__: Blue Circle AoE, invisible after a few seconds.
    - __Dark Geas__: Interact with, don't stand in circles. Immediately followed by clones.
