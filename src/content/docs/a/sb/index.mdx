**Return to Ivalice**, a collaboration with _Final Fantasy Tactics_ / _Final Fantasu XII_ writer Ysaumi Matsuno, is the Level 70 24-man raid series of Stormblood. It was released in three parts: The Royal City of Rabanastre (Patch 4.1), The Ridorana Lighthouse (Patch 4.3) and The Orbonne Monastery (Patch 4.5).

The series is unlocked with the blue quest _Dramatis Personae_ (NPC _Keiten_, Kugane [X:12.2, Y:12.3]).
