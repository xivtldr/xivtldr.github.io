**Alliance Raids** are Final Fantasy XIV's 24-man content that pits players
against a series of bosses. New alliance raids are released with an expansion's
odd patches.

With the exception of _A Realm Reborn's_ _Crystal Tower_ series, all alliance raids are optional unlocks.
