    ___

    - **Knuckle Sandwich** ("_Wait for it.._"): Number of times fist glows determines which marked Circles will explode (starting from Center)
      - Immediately followed by untelegraphed Donut
    - **Banish Storm**: Two sets of three [Exaflares](glossary:exaflare) in indicated direction
    - **Holy**: Spread, **do not overlap**!
    - **Crystalline Thorns / Auroral Uppercut**: Watch for number of times fist glows, indicating which arc will be used.
    - **Banishga IV**: Inward our outward traveling Circle AoE's, indicated by enlarged orbs

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Fafnir the Forgotten">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Sharp Spike (all tanks)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Dark Matter Blast, <mark>L, Multi</mark> Hurricane Wing

      **Baleful Breath**: <mark>Multi</mark> Line Stack

      **Esuna**: Offensive Posture (Out, Concussion)
    :::
    ___

    - **Offensive Posture**: Attacks based on animation
      - _Fafnir raises its tail..._: Front is safe
      - _An intense heat radiates from Fafnir's mouth..._: Untelegraphed Donut
      - _Fafnir makes ready to land..._: Large Out
    - _Tempestuous winds begin blowing through the aery..._
      - Traveling Out or Donut, direction indicated by arrows. Mind lingering tornadoes moving around the arena.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="The Ark Angels">
    :::tank
    _One add will be assigned to your alliance party._

      **TB**: Cloudsplitter (all tanks)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Dominion Slash (EV), Holy (EV), <mark>L</mark> Meteor (TT, if not interrupted), Mighty Strikes (HM)

      **Arrogance Incarnate**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker
    :::
    :::all
    Interrupt **Meteor** (TT, _Ark Angle TT begins incanting..._).

    Interrupt **Mijin Gakure** (HM, after EV's shield is broken). Will wipe raid if not stopped.

    Interrupt **Critical Reaver** (2nd cast, following **Mighty Strikes**).
    :::
    ___

    ### MR, GK, TT

    - Only your assigned add can be damaged
    - **Meikyo Shisui**: Crossing Line AoE's, expanding Circle AoE (flower), Gaze
      - Ends with Conal AoE's into Donut (flower)
    - **Guillotine**: Back of TT is safe. Four hits.

    ### HM and EV

    - Tethers: Move away from clones, they crit
    - Destroy shield and interrupt **Mijin Gakure** or the party wipes
    - **Rampage**: Sequential Line AoE's finishing in large Circle AoE

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Kindred Rage: Shadow Lord">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Unbridled Rage (all tanks)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Flames of Hatred, Cthonic Fury, <mark>L</mark> Doom Arc, Tera Slash (Ultimate)

      **Echoes of Agony**: <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker
    :::
    :::all
    Mitigate _Tera Slash_ (Ultimate) if undergeared.
    :::

    ___

    - **Giga Slash**: Sequential Conals in sword-indicated directions.
      - **Nightfall**: additional 3rd cleave (front/behind).
      - Clone repeats Giga Slash.
    - **Umbra Smash**: Sequential cardinal Line AoEs, expand outward after resolving.
      - Combines with centered Plus (last phase).
    - **Implosion**: Out, Half-Room indicated by larger orb.
      - Clone repeats Implosion.
    - _Hatred spills forth from the Shadow Lord's dark sigils.._
      - Spike direction: Out/Donut.
      - Combined with additional Circle-spike: adds Conal in indicated direction
      - Combined with Square-spike: adds Line AoE in indicated direction
    - **Binding Sigil**: Delayed Circle AoE sets. Stand in last set, move to first once it has exploded.
    - **Dark Nebula**: 4 sequential knockbacks. Position for CW/CCW Knockbacks.
      - Followed by Giga Slash: Nightfall.

  </TabItem>
