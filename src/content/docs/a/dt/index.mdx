**Echoes of Vana'diel**, a collaboration with _Final Fantasy XI_ , is the Level 100 24-man raid series of Dawntrail. It was released in three parts: Jeuno: The First Walk (Patch 7.1), San d'Oria: The Second Walk (Patch 7.3) and Windurst: The Third Walk (Patch 7.5).

The series is unlocked with the blue quest _An Otherworldly Encounter_ (NPC _Hoobigo Messenger_, Tuliyollal [X:13.0, Y:11.6]).
