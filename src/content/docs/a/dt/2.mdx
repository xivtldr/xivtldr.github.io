  <TabItem label="Faithbound Kirin">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Deadly Hold (Towers, each tank)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Stonega IV, Shattering Stomp (Genbu), <mark>Multi</mark> Kirin Captivator
    :::

    ___

    - **Wrought Arms / Synchronized Strike**: Center into Side AoE. Repeats with changed Center orientation
    - **Wringer**: Out into Donut
      - **Double Wringer**: Wrought Arms / Wringer Combo.
    - **Crimson Riddle**: Half-Room, back (glowing tail) or front (fire)
    - **Summon Shijin**
      - Moonbound Genbu: Avoid Spouts, mind Donut
      - Duskbound Byakko: Center Dash, followed by large Circle AoE on endpoint
      - Dawnbound Seiryu: Rotating AoE. Repeats.
      - Sunbound Suzaku: Expanding Circle AoE with Charge through Center
    - **Striking Left/Right**: Large Circle AoE under tethered arm
    - **Synchronized Sequence** / **Smiting (Left/Right) Sequence**: Combos AoE
      - Out/Donut or Center/Sides sequence followed by Large AoE under tethered arm
      - Reverse order for Smiting Left/Right

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Ultima, the Feared / Omega, the One">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Antimatter (Ultima, each tank)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Ion Efflux (Omega), <mark>L</mark> Citadel Buster (Omega)
    :::
    ___

    - **Aft-to-fore Fire / Fore-to-aft Fire**: Half-Room in indicated direction. Delayed resolve, repeats.
    - **Tractor Beam**: Knockback, mind half of small ship (cleaves)
    - **Surface Missile**: Memorize sequential AoE locations
    - **Mana Screen**: Line AoE is now reflected by 90° in angled direction
      - Attack reflects on two screens with next cast, third screen won't be hit.
    - **Chemical Bomb**: Sequential [Proximity Markers](glossary:proximity-marker). Rotate with spawn order.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Kam'lanaut">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Princely Blow (Knockback, each tank)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Enspirited Swordplay, <mark>L, Multi</mark> Transcendent Union
    :::
    :::all
    Mitigate **Transcendent Union**
    :::
    ___

    - **Elemental Blade**: Memorize two imbued elements, indicated by dropping rings around boss
      - **Sublime Elements**: Conals or Line AoE's of imbued elements will expand
      - Three imbued elements in phase 2
    - **Light Blade**: Centered Line AoE followed by alternating Line AoE's
    - **Great Wheel**: Out into Half-Room in front. Boss direction changes after Out.
    - **Crystalline Resonance**: Three sets of one expanding Circle AoE. Crystals hit by corresponding element explode in large Out.
      - 2nd cast: Now has two expanding Circle AoE's
    - **Shield Bash / Illumed Facet**: Only bridge not faced by clones is safe

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Legacy of the Zilart: Eald'narche">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Uranos Cascade (each tank)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: <mark>Multi</mark> Empyreal Vortex
    :::

    ___

    - **Cronos Sling**: Out/Donut indicated by floating cards surrounding boss
      - Followed by Half-Room, side indicated by same cards
    - **Warp**: Move to indicated TP location in corner
      - Followed by large frontal cleave
    - **Gaea Stream**: Traveling Line AoE's. Wait next to first Line Aoe, move into it once it goes off.
      - Two sets in last phase. Move into first, move back, move into first again.
    - **Omega Javelin**: Spread, Circle AoE's have delayed explosion
    - **Duplicate**: Plus
      - **Visions of Paradise**: Two Plus, one will have position swapped as indicated by tether
      - First identify safe spot from static Plus, then resolve moving Plus. One safe spot only.
    - **Ancient Triad**: Proximity Marker or Pull
      - Proximity Marker turns into Out followed by Donut

  </TabItem>
