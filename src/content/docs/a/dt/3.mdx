    ___

    - Hole / Teleport pads in center of each platform.
    - **Cosmic Breath** / **Cosmic Tail**: Switch to lower/top platform.
    - **Cloak of Twilight**: Move to matching platform. Top is Light, Lower is Dark.
    - **Cataclysmic Vortex**: Obey command. Failing reduces HP to 1.
      - Gaze, Move, Don't Move
      - **Gaze with ?**: Look at the boss.
    ### Phase 2
    - **Celestial Trail**: Towers. Random players receive large damage, HP recovery down debuff. Rotate out for second set.
    - **Left/Right Swordscross**: L/R front corner is safe.
    - **Twin Blaze**: Half-Room in indicated direction paired with Donut.
    - **Burst**: Outward traveling, overlapping [Donuts](glossary:donut).
