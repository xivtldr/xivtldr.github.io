  <TabItem label="Ambujam">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Big Wave (DoT)
    :::

    - **Tentacle Dig**: Glowing Tentacle will explode in huge Circle AoE at the end of its path.
      - Both tentacles will explode in later casts.
    - **Toxic Fountain**: Three sets of Circle AoE's. Stand in last set, move into first.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Armored Chariot">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Rail Cannon
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Diffusion Ray
    :::

    - **Articulated Bits**: Death Claws in corner are marked to indicate order in which they fire lasers into center.
      - Boss reflects lasers, creating Conal AoE's.
      - Later casts have additional Claws not hitting the shield.
      - Narrow safe spot next to non-reflecting claws and the created Conal from the shield.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Looming Warden: Kapikulu">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Crewel Slice
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Billowing Bolts
    :::

    - **Spin Out**: Limited controlled spin into direction of pointer.
      - Second cast combines with wide Charge through center, followed by one of the Sides.
    - **Wild Weave**: Boss tethers to either green or yellow rope. All ropes of same color will explode in large Circle AoE's.

  </TabItem>
