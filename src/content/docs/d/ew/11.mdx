    - **Call of the Mountain**: Watch either West or East side for falling snow.

    - **Roar of Albion**: Hide behind ice formation.

    :::

    - **Waxing/Waning Cycle**: Out into Donut or vice versa.
    - **Soul Nebula / Scarecrow Chase**
      - Boss will TP to each portal, order indicated by orbs.
      - Intercardinal Line AoE's from portals.
      - Stand in center, only move to avoid center portal in later casts.
    - **Tenebrism (Glassy-eyed debuff)**: Towers. Face outside of arena when debuff expires.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Drowned King: Cagnazzo">

    :::healer

    - **Antediluvian**: Sequential Circle AoE's from bubbles in order of appearance.
      - Followed by Knockback.
    - **Hydraulic Ram**: Charge to four positions, in sequence.
    - **Voidcleaver**: Conal from each portal in direction of tethered players.
