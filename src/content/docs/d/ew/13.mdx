    - **Hexing Staves / Ruinous Confluence**: Plus from staves.
      - Later casts summon three staves, each disappearing with **Sorcerous Shroud**.
    - **Shadowy Sigil**: Stand on color opposite to boss telegraph.

  <TabItem label="Damcyan Antlion">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Sandblast
    :::

    - **Antlion March**: Sequential Charge. One safe spot on edge of starting position.
      - Mind falling, damaged pillars during **Landslip** Knockback.
    - **Earthen Geyser / Pound Sand**: Stack Marker, move out of resulting quicksand
      - Followed by large Circle AoE on healer.
      - Healer should move to the back, while the party moves to the front.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Memoriate: Durante">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Arcane Edge
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Old Magic
    :::

    - **Duplicitous Battery**: Cascading Circle AoE's traveling inwards.
      - Stand close to edge, move into it once safe.
    - **Forsaken Fount**
      - Orbs explode in large Circle AoE.
      - **Contrapasso**: Orbs will be sliced, each half traveling outwards before exploding.
      - Spawns five orbs in later casts.
    - **Hard Slash**: Wide Conal in front of boss.
    - **Twilight Phase**: Wide center Line AoE.
    - **Dark Impact**: Large Circle AoE.
    - **Death's Journey**: Stand in-between purple lines, away from boss.

  </TabItem>
