<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Face">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Heart On Fire IV
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Temper's Flare
    :::

    - Check your debuff: **Smiley Face (Blue)** or **Frowny Face (Red)**.
      - Debuff changes every time you get hit by a face.
    - **Lines of Fire**: Boss tethers to face statues on the West/East side of the arena.
      - Always stand in a lane opposite your debuff color when the mechanic resolves.
      - Combines with **Off My Lawn**: Knockback, position to get knocked back into correct lane.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Frameworker">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Steel Beam
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Circular Saw
    :::

    - **Leap Forward**: Boss, Print Workers jump in direction they are facing, dropping large AoE Circles at end location.
    - **Omnidimensional Onslaught**: Sequential Conal AoE's in cardinal, intercardinal directions.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="The Big Cheese">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Piercing Missile
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Violent Discharge
    :::

    - **Explosives Distribution**
      - Single Arrow: Bomb explodes in front.
      - Triple Arrows: Bomb explods in back.
    - **Dispense Explosives**: Mines will explode and give Paralysis if touched.
      - Healers, Tanks can clear this to create a safe spot during **Explosives Distribution**.
    - **Left/Right Disassembler** Half-Room. Safe spot is opposite the cast name.

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
