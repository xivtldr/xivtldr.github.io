  <TabItem label="Caustic Grebuloff">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Pox Flail
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Miasmata

      **Necrosis**: Doom debuff, cleanse with Esuna.
    :::

    - **Miasmata**: Circle AoE's will move in direction of wind.
      - Look for gap on the opposite of direction wind is blowing from.
      - Combines with Donut. Resolve Donut first, then move to safe spot.
    - **Cough Up**: Spread into Kiss.
    - **Wave of Nausea**: Donut.

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Peacekeeper">
      :::tank
      **TB**: Elimination
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Decimation
    :::

    - One player on each Cardinal: Spread Markers limit safe spots.
    - **Order To Fire**: Turrets will fire Line AoE's.
      - Combines with **Peacefire**: Move clockwise.
    - **No Future**: Similar to Exaflare. Stand on edge and wait for first set to resolve, then move into new safe spot.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="The Last Mercy: Ra-la">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Pity
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Warm Glow (will cast multiple times when boss reaches low health)

      **Doom (Heal to Full)**: Lifesbreath (if hit by butterflies)
    :::

    - **Prance**: Sequential Large Circle AoE's in boss jump order.
      - Move into last circle, then move into first circle once it goes off.
    - **Lifesbreath**: Alternating Line AoE's.
      - Combines with **Loving Embrace**: Half-Room indicated by glowing wing.

  </TabItem>
