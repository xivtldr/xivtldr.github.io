    - Don't fall asleep.
    - Mind Line AoE's (outside Adds) and **Unite Mare** (kill one to create safe spot).

  <TabItem label="Beatrice">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Hush
    :::
    :::healer
      **Doom (Esuna)**: Eye of Troia
    :::

    - **Eye of Troia**: Gaze, later casts have two sets of two rings.
      - **Toric Void**: Combines with Donut.
    - **Beatific Scorn**: Glowing lines will explode in Circle AoE.
      - They will go off in order of appearance, turning from purple to orange.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Blighted Despot: Scarmiglione">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Firedamp
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Cursed Echo (DoT), Creeping Decay (end of Add phase)
    :::

    - **Blighted Bedevilment**: Position for Knockback into wall.
      - Avoid hitting walls with targeted AoE throughout the fight.
    - **Blighted Bladework**: Huge Circle AoE centered around end of tether.
      - Immediately followed by **Blighted Sweep**: Move behind boss.

  </TabItem>
