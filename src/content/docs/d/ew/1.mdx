      **RW**: Dhrupad (DoT, after every Transmute)

    - Elemental Attacks
      - **Manuysa Fire III**: Donut
      - **Manusya Blizzard III**: 12 Conal AoE's, originating from boss
      - **Manusya Thunder III**: Outward traveling small Circle AoE's
      - **Manusya Bio III**: 180° Cleave in front of boss
    - Transmute Elemental Attacks
      - Orb traveling towards marker will trigger elemental attack at that location

  <TabItem label="Sanduruva">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Isitva Siddhi
    :::

    - **Manusya Berserk**: Stand in gap w/o orb. Large Circle AoE's.
    - **Manusya Confuse**: The real boss will always be the one *not emoting*.
      - Clones will explode in large Circle AoE's, run behind boss.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Harbingers Elect: The Magus Sisters">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Sanduruva (after Delta Attack)
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Samsara
    :::
    :::all 
    Defeat Cinduruva (can res other bosses) → Sanduruva/Minduruva. 
    
    Delta Attacks will stop after Cinduruva is defeated.
    :::

    - **Delta Attack**: As long as Cinduruva is alive, all attacks will be reflected by each boss.
      - _"Glacial ice, entomb our foes!"_: Conals and Line AoE's, paired with proximity markers.
      - _"Thunderous tempest, smite our foes!"_: Circle and Line AoE's, paired with Stack Marker.
      - _"Insatiable flame, consume our foes!"_: Donut into Line AoE's into Donut, Spread.

  </TabItem>
