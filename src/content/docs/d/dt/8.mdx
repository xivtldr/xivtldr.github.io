    ## Troubling Teacups / Tea Awhirl

    Possessed teacups explode in a very large circle AoE.

    - **1 Ghost**: Stand where ghost first spawns

    From here on out, identify the second ghost's relative spawn position to the first and move into these safe-zones:
    - **Opposite**: One Intercardinal direction clockwise, Pink area
    - **Adjacent**: One Cardinal direction clockwise from both cups (e.g. West/North: Move East)
