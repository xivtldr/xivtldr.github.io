<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Herpekaris">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Convulsive Crush
    :::
    :::healer 
      **RW**: Strident Shriek
    :::

    - **Vasoconstrictor / Venomspill**: Multiple Circle AoE's on side of raised arm. Repeats.
      - Stand in center, behind boss, and immediately move to opposite side of raised arm. Immediately move back to middle once Circles go off.
      - Area that will be hit is slightly larger than expected.
    - **Writhing Riot**: Three sequential Conals: One 90° behind, two 210° left and right. Random order.
      - Stand close to boss in line with target circle arrow on one side
      - Move to opposite once side AoE went off

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Deceiver">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Electrowave

      _Upcoming trash after this boss: **Hard Stomp** (Turtle) can be interrupted with _Repose_._
    :::

    - **Bionic Thrash**: Hourglass AoE. 2 Squares indicated by arms will explode
    - **Initialize Androids/Turrets**: Stand in line with glitching android/turret
      - **Surge**: Aim for non-glitched turret

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Innovation's Excess: Ambrose the Undeparted">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Voltaic Slash
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Psychic Wave
    :::
 
    - **Overwhelming Charge**: Half-Room in front
    - **Psychokinesis**: Avoid line of glowing cages
    - **Extrasensory Field**: Don't get pushed into wall
      - Combines with **Overwhelming Charge** - land behind boss
    - **Psychokinesis**: Lance will dash into direction of disappearing red lines
      - Stand next to boss, in-between where two lines bounce off the walls
      - Line AoE's are slightly larger than expected, but melee can safely retain uptime
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
