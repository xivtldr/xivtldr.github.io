  <TabItem label="Ryoqor Terteh">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Frosting Fracas
    :::

    - **Fluffle Up / Cold Feet**: Move into safe-zones once AoE goes off
      - 1st cast: Stand in front of tethered add
      - 2nd cast: Stand in overlap of tethered adds
      - 3rd cast: Stand in-between overlap of circles, in front of tethered adds

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Kahderyor">
    :::healer 
      **RW**: <mark>L</mark> Wind Unbound
    :::

    - Tower Stack Marker turns into Donut or Out
    - Crystal narrow lines are safe-zones, unless they glow yellow
    - Destroy own Crystal Jail. Will kill if not freed after 21s.
    - **Sharpened Sights**: Gaze will be combined with Out and Donut

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Dawn's Elector: Gurfurlur">
    :::healer
      **RW**: Heaving Haymaker, Enduring Glory (scales with missed orbs)
    :::

    Outer wall hurts quite a bit, avoid getting knocked into it.

    - **Stonework**
      - Water: Knockback from side of square AoE
      - Fire: Sequential AoE. Stand next to first AoE, then move into it once it goes off
      - Water and Fire will later combine
      - Wind: Knockback, four tornadoes
    - **Arcane Stomp**: Intercept orbs, each grants damage/healing up
      - **Enduring Glory** can wipe party if enough orbs reached boss

  </TabItem>
