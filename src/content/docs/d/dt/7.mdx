<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Barreltender">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Tender Fury
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Barbed Bellow 
    :::

    - **Heavyweight Needles**: Get in-between largest gap
    - **Tender Drop / Barrel Breaker**: Position yourself toward Cactus without flower. Cacti will explode in large (flowers) and small AoE Circles.
    - **Prickly Left / Right**: Half-Room will expand into wide Conal, stand on center side

  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Anthracite">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Chimney Smack
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Carbonaceous Combustion
    :::

    - Red Circles and Green Squares are connected to cannons on the walls
    - When a bomb falls into a hole, the connected cannons on both sides will shoot out Line AoE's
    - **Anthrabomb**
      - Black arcs spawn Circle AoE's, yellow arcs trigger Line AoE's
      - **Carniflagration**: Combined arcs repeat three times back-to-back

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Inexplicable Entity: The Greatest Serpent of Tural">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Screes of Fury
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Dubious Tulidisaster, Greatest Labyrinth
    :::

    - **Bouncy Council**: Clones shoot out Line AoE in front and behind them
      - 2nd cast: Now spawns large Circle AoE. Move to corner.
      - 3rd cast: Combines both, move near Line AoE clones
    - **Greatest Labyrinth**: Reach blue circle pad
      - Work your way backwards to find correct arrow
    - **Great Torrent**: Stand next to first spawned Circle, wait for it to go off, move into it

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
