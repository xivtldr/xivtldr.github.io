      - Later combines with Line AoE and Spread
    - **Centralized Current / Split Current**:  Sides / Center are safe
    - **Ternary Charge**: Out into Donut

  </TabItem>

    - **Subroutine**: Combines with **Partition / R. Partition**
      - Claw: Line AoE
      - Drone: Donut
      - 6 Glowing Towers: Kill asap. Mind possible Donut, Line AoE, Stack Marker Combo.
      - Blue Circle: Knockback. Mind **Partition / R. Partition**!
    - **Elimination**: Glowing lines will explode in order of appearance
      - Every **Subroutine** after will have Claw fire through Donut. Donut will always go off first.
