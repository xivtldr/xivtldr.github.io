    ## Rings
    Don't try to escape ring.
    - Sequential Line AoE's, dodge Electric Knifes with Donut/Out, Three large Circle AoE's
    - **Serial Torture**: Combines previous mechanics
    - Role-specific
      - Tank: Interrupt **Will Breaker** (Phys Vuln Up). TB follows.
      - Healer: **Death Penalty** (Doom (10s), Esuna)
      - DPS: Soft-enrage. DoT will increase until Hellmaker is killed.

    ## Memento / Memories

    - ___Memories of the slain are made manifest...___
      - Two sets of Line AoE's exploding along the tethers. Stand on untethered shade, then move into tethered shade once it explodes.
    - **_Memories of an enemy bombardment are made manifest..._**
      - Move far away from groups, stand clear of single shades
      - Later combines with Knockback
    - **_Memories of war casualties are made manifest..._**
      - Move to corner opposite of diagonally running shades. Huge Line AoE's in direction of running shades.
      - Melee: Triangle on the floor at center edge has safe-zone. Stand to the left or right of top-half depending on diagonally running shades.
      - Followed by tethered Line AoE's.
