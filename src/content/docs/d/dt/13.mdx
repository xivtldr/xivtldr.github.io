    - **Motion Scanner**: Acts like Acceleration Bomb. Don't move or attack when red Line AoE passes you.

    :::healer
      **RW**: Ripples of Gloom
    :::

    - **Mortifying Flesh**: Boss dashes out of arena, then back in. Avoid, repeats twice after 1st cast.
      - Followed by Knockback from center, resolves last.
    - **Basic Vomit**: Conal in front.
    - **Bodyweight Exorcism**: Sequential Towers & Stack Marker.
      - Move from tower to tower.

  </TabItem>
    <TabItem label="Masterful Manipulator: Malphas">
    :::tank
      **TB**: Shadow Play
    :::
    :::healer
      **RW**: Rubbish Disposal
    :::

    - **Void Dark**: Half-Room in front.
    - **Scrap Meddle**: Large puppets do long Conal, small puppets spawn Circle AoE's
      - **Puppet Pair**: Activates both large and small puppets at the same time. Followed by **Void Dark**.
      - **Puppet Mastery**: Boss will move puppets around. Move with large puppet.
    - **String Up** (Play button): Keep moving

  </TabItem>
