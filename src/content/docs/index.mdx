## Welcome!

TLDR guides are straight to the point - you don't need instructions on how to
handle [Stack Markers](glossary:stack) or Towers. But you might want to know
when they have a surprise twist only used in this one obscure duty you haven't
run in years.

This site is meant to be a companion for your duty roulette - most pages can be read mid-fight. And if you are new to the game, you might appreciate these quick duty guides as primers that don't spoil the visual spectacle of a story fight.

Access the search with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> / <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> or browse by content-type.
Boss names are hidden to avoid spoilers.

_Due to the nature of the format, XIV TLDR is focused on casual content only.
For high-end content, check out the excellent [wtfdig.info](https://wtfdig.info/)._
