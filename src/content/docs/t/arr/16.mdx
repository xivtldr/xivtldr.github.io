## Ascian: Nabriales

:::tank

**TB**: <mark>L</mark> Spark (can be doubled or tripled)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: After orb phase (scales with missed orbs),<mark>L</mark> Quake III, Meteor Towers
:::

:::all
Do not use LB in first phase.
:::

---

- Grab orbs. Alternate black and red orbs
- Void Portal
  - Continuously run away
  - With only a few seconds of Blight debuff remaining, use any Buffs you have
  - This includes Sprint, Invuln, shields, damage ups and so on
  - At the end of the debuff you will get sucked in and **all buffs will have their duration drastically increased**
- Tower phase
  - Only tanks should stand in Towers
  - DPS the **Aetherial Tear**. Use Melee DPS LB
