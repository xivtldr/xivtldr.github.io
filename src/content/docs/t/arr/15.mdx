# Ultros & Typhon

:::tank
_One boss per tank. Watch out for Fungah (Typhon) - if you get pushed out of the arena, you will instantly lose aggro._

**TB**: Grind (Typhon)
:::

:::healer
**Severe Snort**: Stand close to boss and use Surecast as a fail safe to avoid potential party wipe.

:::

:::all
Getting knocked off the arena results in a stun. You can jump up after Stun wears off. If everyone gets knocked off, the party wipes.
:::

---

- Focus Ultros
  - DPS will get turned into Imps
  - Stand in front of Ultros and soak **Aqua Breath** at least twice
  - This will get you the debuff _Wet Plate_, which is needed to stop Typhon's **Severe Snort**.

:::deadly
When Typhon starts glowing and casts **Severe Snort**, use **Imp Punch** to interrupt him. If you don't do this, Typhon will knock back everyone.
:::

- Watch out for Typhon's Knockback attacks
  - **Fungah**: Frontal Cone
  - **Fungahhh**: Rear Cone
  - **Snortsault**: Front and Rear, rotating 360°
