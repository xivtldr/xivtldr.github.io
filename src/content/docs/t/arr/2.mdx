# Lord of Crags: Titan

:::tank
_Face Titan away from party. Titan will do a Cleave attack in your direction._

:::

:::healer
**RW**: Ultimate (after his Heart is destroyed)

_Ultimate: A big room-wide attack, usually performed after a phase ends. These attacks often don't have cast-bars._
:::

---

- Proximity Marker: Flashing Circle in the Center of the arena
  - Always get distance from these markers. They will deal more damage the closer you are.
- Immediately after, the red outer part of the arena will disappear. Don't stand in it!
- **Weight of the Land**: Get out of glowing circles
- **Landslide**: Line AoE into a targeted players' direction. Move out of it!
- After another jump, **Titan's Heart** will be targetable. Destroy it.
- Some people will be put into rocks. Free them by attacking the rocks.
