## The Lord of Levin: Ramuh

:::tank
**TB**: Shock Strike (untelegraphed)

_Collecting 3 orbs will give you Surge Protection, lowering the amount of damage received. **Do not grab 4 or more**_.
:::

:::all
Do not stand with the tank.
:::

---

- **Chaotic Strike**: 60s stun (_Terror_)
  - Cleansed by Electric attacks
  - If geared you can stand close to Ramuh, as **Thunderspark** (Out) will follow
  - Other party members targeted by Electric Circles can drop their AoE on you
- Kill adds before **Judgement Bolt** finishes cast
  - **Judgement Bolt** will wipe the party if any add is alive
  - Any add spawn after the Ultimate can be ignored
- **Rolling Thunder**: Tethers two players, inflicting steady damage if any action is used
  - One afflicted player must collect three orbs to break the tether
