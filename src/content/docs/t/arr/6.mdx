## Lady of the Vortex: Garuda

- Hard-mode Garuda has all attacks of [Normal mode Garuda](./3) with only a few changes in Phase 2
  - Large green Circle AoE's will spawn-in short-lived tornadoes. Avoid.
  - Two adds will tether to her. One gives her damage up, the other regenerates her health
  - These adds are easily killed, but you can also just focus on Garuda
