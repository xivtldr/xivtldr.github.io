## Lord of Crags: Titan

:::tank

**TB**: Mountain Buster
:::

:::healer

**RW**: <mark>Multi</mark> Tumult (Titan will stomp the ground multiple times)

**Esuna**: Use Esuna to remove the debuff _Damage Down_ on the player that was trapped in a rock.
:::

- Hard-mode Titan has all attacks of [Normal mode Titan](./2) with a few additions
- **Bomb Boulders**: Bombs spawn with two possible patterns
  - in a circle at the edge of the arena and one in the middle
  - in groups of three in a straight line
  - Wait next to the bomb or bomb row that spawned in last
  - As the first bomb or bomb row explodes, move into it. This is now a safe-spot.
  - You will often see this mechanic involving sequential, delayed explosions in future fights. It is always resolved the same way.
