## Lord of the Inferno: Ifrit

- Hard-mode Ifrit has all attacks of [Normal mode Ifrit](./1) with only two changes
  - Eruptions from cracked circles will now follow a player, for a total of three explosions
  - He now has 3 friends, which will spawn at the edge of the arena, then dash across it one after another
  - Simply stay on the side of the first or last clone and you will be fine
