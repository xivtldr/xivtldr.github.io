## Hydra

:::tank
_Both tanks should stand in front for TB._

**TB**: Triumvirate (less damage if more players are hit - share with off-tank)

:::

:::healer
_Breath attacks inflict uncleansable Infirmity (healing done on target is reduced)._
:::

:::all
Stay at melee or max range to avoid **Radiant Breath**.
:::

---

- **White Breath**: Frontal Cone, large damage.
- **Radiant Breath**: Stay at melee or max range, hits anyone in medium range.
- **Fear Itself**: Donut.
