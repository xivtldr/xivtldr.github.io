# Lord of the Inferno: Ifrit

:::tank
_Face Ifrit away from party. Ifrit will do a Cleave attack in your direction._
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Infernal Nail (if all nails are destroyed)
:::

---

- Don't stand near tank
- **Vulcan Burst**: Untelegraphed Knockback
- **Eruption**: Move out of large cracks
- **Infernal Nail**: Three nails spawn. Kill them asap or the party wipes.
- **Radiant Plume**: Fire puddles will spawn in the cente or around the arena.
  - Move out of them before they explode.
- **Limit Break (LB)**: Remember to use your DPS LB!
