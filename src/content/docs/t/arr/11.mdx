# Lord of the Whorl: Leviathan

:::tank
_Split tanking of tail and head._

**TB**: Dread Tide (head)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Tidal Roar (head)
:::

:::all
**Magic DPS** can only attack **head**, **Physical Ranged DPS** can only attack the **tail**.

PRIORITIZE **Gyre Spumes**! They will drain the elemental converter, leading to a party wipe!
:::

## Phase 1

- **Kill Gyre Spume orbs asap**
  - Elemental Converter at the back of the ship needs at least 30 out of 100 power (check duty bar)
- Activate **Elemental Converter** as soon as Leviathan becomes untargetable
