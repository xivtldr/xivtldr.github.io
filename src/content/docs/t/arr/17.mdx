## Gilgamesh & Enkidu

:::tank
_One boss per tank._

**TB**: Bitter End (Gilgamesh, untelegraphed in P2), <mark>Multi</mark> Sword Dance
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Tornadoes (if not removed by chickens), Moonlight (P2, HP < 75%), Dragonstrike (if Dragon heads are left alive)

**Enchain**: Stretch out tether
:::

---

### Phase 1

- Focus Enkidu
- Tethers: Run close to each other
- Chickens, run through tornadoes to remove them

### Phase 2

- **Enchain**: Attack chain and free healer
- Kill Dragon Heads
- Electric Orbs will explode
