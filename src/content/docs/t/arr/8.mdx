# King of Moogles: Good King Moggle Mog XII

:::tank
**TB**: Moogle Thrust (Rogue), Twin Pom Meteor (shared)

_Twin Pom Meteor is a shared tankbuster. Both tanks must stand in the red circle._
:::

:::healer

**RW**: Pom Holy (Conjurer, King), Memento Moogle (Phase 2)
:::

## Phase 1

- Small Moogles don't have a lot of health and you won't encounter every mechanic - in order of appearance:
  - Out, Large Conal, Spread
  - Raidwide, Big Conal
  - Towers (at least one player must stand in each circle), Tankbuster

# Phase 2

- The King will summon all Moogles again, this time they can't be targeted
- Their attacks change. Only two might surprise you.
- Watch out for the big circular AoE's
  - Three of them will spawn and cover the entire arena
  - Stand on the edge of the first circle, within the third circle that appeared
  - Move into the first circle once it explodes
  - Prepare to dodge a large Conal directly after
- The Conjurer may cast Mog Stone III
  - Two large rings of AoE will cover the entire arena
  - The center section will explode first
  - Stand in the outer ring, then move into the center once it's safe
