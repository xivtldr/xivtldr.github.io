## Lady of the Vortex: Garuda

:::tank
_Face Garuda away from party. Garuda will do a Cleave attack directed at a random player._

**TB**: Slipstream (Interrupt)

:::

:::healer
**RW**: Mistral Song (Phase 1, hits everyone not hiding behind pillar), Ultimate (after she flies away for the 3rd time)
:::

---

- Don't stand next to the tank, he will get targeted with a Cleave attack!
- **Whicked Wheel**: No cast-bar. Garuda will deal damage in a circle area directly below her feet.
  - From here on out, we will refer to this as "Out".
- When Garuda disappears, stand behind one of the 4 rock pillars.
  - When she reappears, make sure the pillar is blocking her "line-of-sight"
  - From here on out, we will refer to this type of mechanic as "Meteor"
- Add phase: **Razor Plumes** will spawn. Kill them fast, as they can damage the stone pillars.
- Slipstream: A large hit directed at the tank (TB).
  - Interrupt this cast!
  - Glowing orange cast-bars can be interrupted with the role-actions **Interject** (Tanks) or **Head Graze** (Physical Ranged DPS). Stuns often work too.

### Phase 2

- **Eye of the Storm**: A large circular AoE. Only the center is safe.
  - From here on out, we will refer to this as "Donut"
  - Usually Donuts disappear again, but this one stays for the rest of the fight.
- **Mistral Song**: Will now hit anyone in front of her. Get behind Garuda.
  - When a boss hits one half of the arena, we refer to it as "Half-Room Cleave"
