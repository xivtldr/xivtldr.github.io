## Mark I Anti-Eikon Warmachina: The Diamond Weapon

:::tank
_Straight Line Cleaving auto-attacks. Stay clear of party_.

**TB**: Auri Doomstead
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Diamond Rain, Outrage
:::

:::all
Use Teleport pads to switch platforms as needed. Gives debuff for 4s, restricting TP pad use.
:::

---

- **Adamant Purge**: Full Cleave of platform indicated by glowing claws
  - Can also be a Stack Marker. Ensure everyone is on right platform.
- **Auri Arts**: Zig-zag Line AoE, followed by Half-Room
  - Stand between two Line AoE, adjust for Half-Room
  - Second _Auri Arts_: Center Line AoE followed by Out.
- **Adamant Sphere**: Towers on **both** platforms.
