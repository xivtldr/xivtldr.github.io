## Warrior of Light: Elidibus

:::tank
**TB**: The Bitter End

**LB**: After Intermission, Ultimate Crossover - time with _"Transcend your limits and weather the gathering storm!"_"
:::

:::healer
**RW**: <mark>1HP</mark> Terror Unleashed (**Heal everyone to full within 15s**), <mark>L</mark> Elddragon Dive
:::

:::all
Using DPS LB will make Elidibus invulnerable for 8s. Avoid by using it while he is casting another ability.
:::

---

- **Coruscant Saber**
  - Rings surrounding sword: Donut. Otherwise Out.
- **Absolute Fire III**: Pyretic at end of countdown.
- **Absolute Blizzard III**: Move at end of countdown.
- **Imbued Absolute Blizzard/Fire III**: Untelegraphed delayed versions.
- **Sword of Light**: Triangle, sides are safe.
- **Summon Wyrm**: Half-Room Cleave from Bahamut.

### Phase 2

:::all
Everyone needs to correctly finish the **Active Time Maneuver**. Smash any button. Bar progress is misleading - you can't fail as long as you hit one button repeatedly. FAILURE CAUSES PARTY-WIPE.
:::

- **Ultimate Crossover**: Boss gets LB4. Tank LB needed.
- **Adds**: Untargetable.
  - Towers, Donut/Out, Expanding Circle AoE, Flare, Stack Marker
- **Radiant Braver**: Targeted Cone directed at 3 players
