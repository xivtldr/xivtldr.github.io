## Yojimbo

:::tank
_Embodiment adds ned to be separated. Both have small cleaving TB's._

**TB**: Wakizashi, Bitter End

:::

:::healer
**RW**: Metta-giri, Dragon Night (when Dragon's Head hit end of arena), <mark>1HP</mark> Zanma Zanmai
:::

---

- **Gekko**: Spread, Circles turn into Donut
- **Tiny Song**: Kiss
- **Electrogenetic Force** (Purple Orbs): Safe Spot is on Tank
- **Enchain**: Tank/healers.
  - Proximity-Tether, resulting chains are easier to break for DPS the further away you are.
- **Epic Stormsplitter**: Wait for it to go off, then move into narrow center line.
