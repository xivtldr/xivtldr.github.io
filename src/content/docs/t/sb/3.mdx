## Shinryu

:::tank

**TB**: <mark>Multi</mark> Akh Morn (both tanks), Death Sentence (Adds)

:::

:::healer
_Both tanks are hit by auto-attacks._

**RW**: Ice Storm, Hellfire, Judgment Bolt, Protostar (Ultimate), Earthen Fury

**Akh Morn**: Tethered player will also receive <mark>Multi</mark> AoE (Phase 2 only)

**Esuna**: Judgment Bolt (RNG Paralysis)
:::

:::all
**Corrupted Aether Bar**: Primal Attacks happen when bar fills.
:::

---

- **Tidal Wave**: Large Knockback from West or East.
- **Hypernova**: Stack Marker. Stand in water puddle.
- **Earth Breath**: Earthshaker, drop on edge.
- Room-wide attacks
  - **Hellfire**: Stand in water.
  - **Judgment Bolt**: Do **not** stand in water.
- Add phase: Adds leave behind Proximity Marker

### Phase 2

- Tail Slam: Avoid red square. Destroy Tail for huge damage to Shinryu
  - Cracked Squares will vanish after Slam, shrinking the platform
- **Aerial Blast**: Three small, one large Knockback originating from Vortex
- Green Diamond Marker: Indicates Tail Slam location. Rarely seen nowadays.
