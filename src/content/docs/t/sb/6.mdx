## Rathalos

:::tank
_Rathalos can't be tanked. **Garula** (Add) needs to be tanked._

**TB**: Cleave (Garula Add)

:::

:::healer
_Healers can't heal in final phase. Player mounting Rathalos will have constant Bleed debuff._

**RW**: Roar

**Esuna**: Targeted Circle AoE (Burn), Sleep (Sheep), Paralysis (Coeurl)
:::

:::all
Rathalos can't be tanked. Watch party list (A next to job symbol).

All attacks are untelegraphed. Watch for animations.

Healers can't heal in final phase. Use Duty Action **Mega Potion** (10 Stacks).
:::

---

- Legs forward, Fireball in mouth: Hourglass Conals towards aggro target.
- Head looks behind: Cleave on indicated side. Alternates.
- Head lowered, wings spread: Charge
- Head lowered, wings raised: Conal in front

## Add phase

- 1 Player: Interact with blue circle, keep smashing buttons

:::deadly
Hide behind dead Garula
:::

## Flying phase

- Destroy tail when Rathalos falls down
- Tells are more difficult to decipher now
  - Watch his positioning towards the aggro target
  - If close to Rathalos, expect Frontal Conal and Knockback
  - If far away expect Charge
  - Now also includes Stack Marker
