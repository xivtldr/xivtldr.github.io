## Auspice: Suzaku

:::tank

**TB**: Cremate, <mark>Multi</mark> Phantom Flurry

:::

:::healer
**RW**: Ashes to Ashes (Adds, if not killed in time), Screams of the Damned, Scarlet Melody (scales with meter), Southron Star
:::

---

- **Eternal Flame**: Kill phoenixes asap
  - **Group them up in center**, watch for Conal
- 8 Green Circles: One player in each. Face direction of arrow.
- **Ruthless Refrain**: Knockback, no indicator other than castbar.
- **Phantom Flurry**: TB is followed by Half-Room where tank stood.
- Colored quadrants (_"Harken to my melody"_)
  - Phoenix flies around arena, hitting colored runes
  - Same colored squares explode
  - Next cast has 4 squares exploding in sequence
