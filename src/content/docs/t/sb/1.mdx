## Lord of the Revel: Susano

:::tank
_"How our hearts sing in the chaos!": Pick up metallic flower. Active Time Maneuver._

**TB**: Assail (untelegraphed), Stormsplitter
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Ukehi (can hit twice)

- **Dark Cloud**: Clouds cast Line AoE that inflicty Paralysis.
- _"How our hearts sing in the chaos!"_: Destroy sword. Intercept blue orbs.
