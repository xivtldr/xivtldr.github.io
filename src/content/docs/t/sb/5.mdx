## The Lady of the Moon: Tsukuyomi

:::tank

**TB**: Torment Unto Death (Conal)

:::

:::healer
**RW**: Reprimand, Nightbloom (Stun), <mark>Multi</mark> Zenos (Add), Ultimate (after Adds), <mark>L</mark> Antitwilight/Perilune, Dance of the Dead

**Lunacy** (P3): <mark>Multi</mark> Stack Marker
:::

---

- **Zashiki-Asobi**: Shiva Circles
  - Combines with **Steel of the Underworld**: Large targeted Conal AoE
  - Combines with Stack Marker in P2
- Add phase: Mind Zenos' Knockback.
- **Selenomancy**
  - Each colored side gives debuff, stack count indicated by moon phases
  - Swap sides before stack hits 5 or you get Doom (cleansed by swapping side within 5s)
  - Combines with Donut and Line Stack
