# Knight in Black: Golbez

:::tank
**TB**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Void Meteor (both tanks)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Binding Cold, **<mark>L, Multi</mark>** Black Fang
:::

---

- **Crescent Blade**: <u>Half-Width</u> cleave on side of sword.
  - Repeats after Lingering Spark
- **Shadow Crescent**: <u>Half-Width</u> cleave on side of sword **followed by <u>Out</u>**.
- Tower Phase: Healer has to position in-between towers. Multiple players per tower.
  - Crosshair: Bait Line AoE away before moving out of proximity markers.
- **Gale Sphere**:
  - Find safe area from first two clones.
  - Move to safe area for last two clones.
- **Void Stardust**: <u>Exaflare</u>, stand and move next to closest arrow.
