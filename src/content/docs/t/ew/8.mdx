## False Manusya: Asura

:::tank
**TB**: Cutting Jewel
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Lower Realm, Ephemerality
:::

---

- **Ephemerality**: Clones will perform Out.
- **Iconography**: Big Clone repeats boss casts.
  - **Pedestal Purge**: Out.
  - **Wheel of Deincarnation**: Donut.
  - **Bladewise**: Line AoE. Clone Line AoE is HUGE, go to sides.
  - Clone get's tp'd to random corner the next time Iconography is cast.
- **Six-bladed Khadga**: Six Half-Room cleaves in order of appearance.
  - Always traveling clockwise or counterclockwise.
  - **Stand behind boss, alternate moving left/right depending on her last hit.**
  - Last three are a repeat of the first three.
- **The Face of Wrath** / **Delight**: Half-Room matching the flashed mask's color explodes.
