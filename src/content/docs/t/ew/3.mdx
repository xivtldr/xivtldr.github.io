# The Endsinger

:::tank
**TB**: Hubris (both tanks, cleaves)

**LB**: After add dies (_"4 seconds until oblivion is sealed..."_).
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Elegeia, Elegeia Unforgotten, Katastrophe, Ultimate Fate, Telos
:::

---

- **Planets**: Move opposite of slow moving planet.
  - **Elegeia Unforgotten**: Use knockback to position yourself away from second explosion.
  - Tether shows which explosion goes off first.
- **Elenchos**:
  - Mouth glows: sides are safe.
  - Eyes tear: center is safe.
- **Ekstasis**: Center Edge of boss is safe.
- **Add phase**: Use **<u>DPS LB</u>**.
  - LB will refresh after add dies for tank usage.
