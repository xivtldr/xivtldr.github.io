- **Algedon**: Opposite front edge of boss is safe.

#### Snakes

- Go into **one quadrant** of the arena. Divide it roughly into 4 squares.
- Rotate **only the safezone or AoE in this quadrant** 90° in your head.

```
o = Snek AoE
x o  [rotated 90°   o o   [bottom row
x o  to the left]   x x   is safe]
```

#### Astral Eclipse

- Move West.
- Check patterns in the sky; order is West, South, East

```
Possible patterns

       *   |  *     *  |     *
 *  *  *   |           |  *  *  *
 *         |  *     *  |     *

 West/East |   Center  |  Intercardinals ... are safe
```
