# Autarch of Flame: Rubicante

:::tank
**TB**: Shattering Heat, Dualfire (huge conal AoE)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Inferno, Ghastly Torch (stacking, Greater Flamesent add), **<mark>L</mark>** Blazing Rapture
:::

---

- **Ordeal of Purgation**: Stand on line next to orange line.
  - With arrows: Stand on edge of purple ring on orange line.
- Adds: Kill Greater Flamesent asap.
- **Flamerake**: Memorize arrow direction.
  - Stand to the side, then move inwards after Line AoE goes off.
