## Grasping Eternity: Necron

:::tank
**TB**: Blue Shockwave (massive 90° tracking AoE)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Fear of Death, Grand Cross, Neutron Ring, Darkness of Eternity
:::

---

_Hands: Line AoE in indicated direction_

- **Cold Grip**: Side AoEs immediately followed by 2/3 AoE on side of glowing hand
- **Soul Reaping/Aetherblight**
  - Hollow Ring: Delayed Donut on boss
  - Filled Ring: Delayed Out on boss
- **Grand Cross**: Intercardinal is safe for first beam, after that every Cardinal is safe.
- Relentless Reaping: Four casts of Soul Reaping (Donut/Out)
  - Bottom diamond shows which stored attacks goes off
  - If its 1 or 3, it will start with first AoE pattern that was shown
  - If it is 2 or 4 its the opposite of that pattern
  - Pattern will always be 2x Out → Donut or 2x Donut → Out
