# Siege Engine: Doomtrain

:::tank
**TB**: Lightning Burst (both), Aether Surge (tracking conal)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Unlimited Express, **<mark>L</mark>** _'You hear the scream of metal...'_, Shockwave (no cast bar)

**Derailment Siege/Battering Arms**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Stack Marker
:::

---

- **Lightning Express**: Stand in front of high laser
  - Later resolved by getting to high/low ground
- **Windpipe**: <u>Knockback</u>, 1/3 AoE in front of boss
- **Turret Crossing**: Turrets will fire after every boss mechanic. Look for objects blocking line-of-sight.
- **Head-on Emission**
  - Crackling Lighting: Use TP to move up
  - Headlamp glows orange: Get to low ground
- **Arcane Revelation**: Large Circle AoE
  - First is in front of boss
  - Every other follows the path shown by diamond on floor
