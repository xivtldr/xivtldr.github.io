# Sovereign of Naught: Enuo

:::healer
**RW**: Meteorain, Almagest

**Dimension Zero**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Line Stack
:::

---

- **Naught Grows**: Out from indicated portal
  - After add phase: Out also from boss
  - Out can hit boss and portal at same time
  - Two arrows will indicate two large circle AoEs from portals
- **Gaze of the Void**: Rotating Conals in order of orb spawns. Move behind.
- **Vacuum**: End points explode in Circle AoE
  - Biggest gap between two end points is safe
