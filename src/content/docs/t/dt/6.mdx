# The White Wraith: Guardian Arkveld

:::tank
**TB**: During <u>Tower</u> phase
:::

:::healer
**RW**: _'Guardian Arkveld brings its might to bear!'_, Roar
:::

---

_Mega Potions are optional, as heals still work._

**This boss has no cast bar. Watch the animations.**

- _Charge_: Followed up by AoE
  - White: Quick Line AoE, Sides are safe
  - Red: Quick Line AoE, Center is safe
- _Line AoE with arrows_: Travels down the arena
  - If it strikes red clouds, another traveling Line AoE follows
- _Three Charge telegraph_: Circle AoE travel outwards, turning into <u>Donut</u>
- _Guardian Arkveld gathers energy..._: <u>Exaflares</u>. Crystal formations explode in Circle AoE.
