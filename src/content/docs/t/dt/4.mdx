:::tank

:::

**RW**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Valorous Ascension, Thorned Catharsis

---

- **Alexandrian Thunder IV**: Out, Donut or vice versa
- **Power Break**: Half-Room indicated by glowing sword
- **Holy Hazard**: Sequential Conals to the Side and Center
- **Thunder Slash**: Donut, Out with 6 sequential Conals
- **Roseblood Bloom**: Illuminated Conals will explode if circle AoE touches them
  - Later combines with _Power Break_ and _Alexandrian Thunder IV_
  - Traveling orb will turn segment into illuminated conal
