# Queen Eternal

:::tank
**TB**: Prosecution of War
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Aethertithe (DoT), Virtual Shift, Royal Domain, **<mark>Multi</mark>** Royal Banishment

- **Aethertithe**: Three <u>Conals</u> indicated by distorted grid.
  - Will combine with <u>Donut</u> after second _Aethertithe_
- **Virtual Shift (1)**
  - Movable Line AoE, <u>Knockback</u>, <u>Donut</u>, <u>Knockback</u>
- **Virtual Shift (2)**
  - Position in front of wall holes
  - Avoid sequential <u>Half-Width</u> **Legitimate Force**
- **Absolute Authority**
  - **Use DPS LB3 before cast ends**
  - DPS: Drop flare at edge
  - Followed by <u>Kiss</u>, and alternating large circle AoEs
  - DPS & Tanks: <u>Acceleration Bomb</u>, look away from Healer
  - Healer: <u>Gaze</u>, go to nearest edge
  - **LB3 refreshes, reserve for Healer usage**
- Final phase: Mind alternating <u>Conals</u>, <u>Donut</u>
