## Queen Eternal

:::tank
**TB**: Prosecution of War
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Aethertithe (DoT), Virtual Shift, Royal Domain, **<mark>Multi</mark>** Royal Banishment
:::

---

- **Aethertithe**: Three Conals indicated by distorted grid.
  - Will combine with Donut after second _Aethertithe_
- **Virtual Shift (1)**
  - Movable Line AoE, Knockback, Donut, Knockback
- **Virtual Shift (2)**
  - Position in front of wall holes
  - Avoid sequential Half-Room **Legitimate Force**
- **Absolute Authority**
  - **Use DPS LB3 before cast ends**
  - DPS: Drop flare at edge
  - Followed by Kiss, and alternating large circle AoEs
  - DPS & Tanks: Acceleration Bomb, look away from Healer
  - Healer: Gaze, go to nearest edge
  - **LB3 refreshes, reserve for Healer usage**
- Final phase: Mind alternating Conals, Donut
