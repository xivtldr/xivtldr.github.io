# King of Resolve: Zoraal Ja

:::tank
**TB**: Patricidal Pique (magic), Bitter Reaping (magic)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Soul Overflow, Dawn of an Age, Actualize

**Duty's Edge**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Stack Marker
:::

---

#### Phase 1

- **Double-edged Swords**: Sequential Half-Width
- **Vorpal Trail**: Traveling inwards, then outwards
  - Mind _Double-edged Swords_ cast

#### Phase 2

- **Smithing Circuit**: Out or Donut

**Dawn of An Age**

- **Sync**: Outer platforms show incoming pattern on main platform
- **Gateway/Forged Track**: Line AoE shoots out at end of tether
  - Platform with _big crossing X_: Line AoE will always hit where swords are first pointing to

- **Half Full**: Half-Width, can combine with _Smithing Circuit_ (In/Donut)

- **Gateway/Forged Track (2nd)**:
  - Locate _big crossing X_, _move out_ of line where swords are first pointing to
  - Adjust for other pattern by _moving to_ line where swords are first pointing to
