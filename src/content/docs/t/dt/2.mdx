## King of Resolve: Zoraal Ja

:::tank
**TB**: Patricidal Pique (magic), Bitter Reaping (magic)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Soul Overflow, Calamity's Edge, Dawn of an Age, Actualize

**Duty's Edge**: **<mark>Multi</mark>** Stack Marker
:::

---

### Phase 1

- **Double-edged Swords**: Sequential Half-Room
- **Vorpal Trail**: Traveling inwards, then outwards
  - Mind _Double-edged Swords_ cast

### Phase 2

- **Smithing Circuit**: Out or Donut

**Dawn of An Age**

- **Sync**: Outer platforms show incoming pattern on main platform
- **Gateway/Forged Track**: Line AoE shoots out at end of tether
  - Platform with _big crossing X's_: Move to line adjacent to swords

- **Half Full**: Half-Room, can combine with _Smithing Circuit_.

- **Gateway/Forged Track (2nd)**:
  - Locate _big crossing X's_, move to line adjacent to swords
  - Adjust for other pattern - move into line with swords
  - Repeat for second set
