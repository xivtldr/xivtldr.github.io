## Lord of the Hive: Ravana

:::tank
_TB's can damage the wall. Don't tank Ravana next to a wall._

_Off-tank is targeted with Line AoE after Pillars of Heaven._

**TB**: Periodic cleaving attacks (untelegraphed)
:::
:::healer
**RW**: Bloody Fuller (scales with any swords left behind by Adds)
:::

:::all
_Ravana gains a stack of damage up and changes his stance every time the Bloodlust bar fills._
:::

---

- **Prelude to Slaugther**: Out, after 1st cast followed by Line AoE, Donut
- **Slaugther**: Behind is safe
- **Adds**: Kill all before they finish casting. Casts leave behind swords, which scale up Ultimate damage
- **Bloody Fuller** (Ultimate): Breaks parts of wall, don't get knocked off from this point onward
- **Pillars of Heaven**: Proximity marker with Knockback
- **Swift Slaughter**: Line AoE in indicated order, followed by Donut then Out
