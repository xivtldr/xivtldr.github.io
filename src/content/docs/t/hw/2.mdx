# Lord of the Mists: Bismarck

:::tank
_Keep big adds apart from each other._

**TB**: Big adds (cleaving, untelegraphed)
:::

:::healer
**RW**: Bismarck's Back (small DoT), Lightning Strikes (Thunder weather, if bubbles are killed)
:::

:::all
_Island Integrity Bar: If it hits zero, the party wipes. When Bismarck prepares to slam the island with Cetacean Rage activate the generator in the center._
:::

---

- Small Adds: Line AoE has Knockback. Don't get pushed out the arena.
- Dragon Killers: Use when ready, then climb onto Bismarck and burn down his Citin Carapace.
- Vapor Bubble: Note the weather.
  - Don't destroy bubbles during _Thunder_ or they will target a random player with a lightning strike.
  - If they are _not_ killed during _Rain_, they will gain _Damage Up_
  - Most people ignore this mechanic. Try to spread to minimize the chance of getting hit by Lightning Strikes.
- If Bismarck is somehow still alive, mind the center, which now gets targeted with an untelegraphed Circle AoE.
